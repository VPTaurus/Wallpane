# Pictures of Wallpane

These screenshots and `wall.mp4` are made by `npm run screenshots` (`scripts/screenshots.js`): Wallpane photographed
with free stock media from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) (free to use under the
[Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/), no credit needed). With thanks to the people who made them:

- Videos: Benjamin Eriksen (ocean waves on a beach), Tom Fisk (waves from above), Timo Volz (Taipei at sunset),
  cottonbro studio (blue and red ink), Engin Akyurt (coloured ink), T Honkamies (northern lights), Magda Ehlers
  (jellyfish), Brixiv (fireplace), Carol McCormick (waterfall), Nui MALAMA (neon in a puddle), Erhan Dayı (clouds over
  mountains), Ambient_Nature_Atmosphere (Milan at night).
- Photos: Francesco Ungaro (mountain and lake), eberhard grossgasteiger (mountains reflected at sunset), Willian
  Justen de Vasconcellos (dunes), Thanh Luu (cherry tree).

The media files themselves are not in this repository (the licence does not allow passing them on); see
ROADMAP G1 for how they were chosen.
